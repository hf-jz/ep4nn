Evidence-gated structural growth

What should make a small brain grow?

A 302-neuron worm wiring, an equilibrium network, and one rule for adding neurons: grow only when two situations the world calls different get the same answer. On the measured worm graph the rule refuses, and the measurement that justifies the refusal is the result.

Prerequisite result

The measured worm readout answers along one direction, so capacity is not the binding constraint

Best of forty-five operating points
0.0397 readout spread, below the growth gate of 0.05, at row sum 0.5 with a gain of two on the interneuron class. No point of the grid reaches the gate.
Collision at that point
0.9979 is the smallest largest absolute cosine between two context answers that must differ, against a gate of 0.85; at the best point the answers span a cone of 1.45 degrees with 99.95 percent of their energy in one singular direction.
Growth decisions, 1200 ticks
90 / 90 growth attempts rolled back across three declared readouts, zero kept, and 302 neurons before and after. The verdict re-settles the situations after growth, because a comparison against stored answers cannot see the growth at all: with it, growth moves the collision by +1.0e-4 at best, that is it worsens, against a margin of 0.02 and a drift floor of exactly zero. The same locomotion readout gated on an aggregate spread instead refuses all 1200 ticks, which is what a gate on the wrong quantity costs.

0.529065 certified contraction of the settling majorant at the library's default efficacy and 0.931159 at the declared operating point, certified by Kolmogorov's inequality with outward rounding, against a global worst-case bound of 88.0 that certifies nothing; worst value 0.941 over a 12-state scanned domain.

The contract

Five gates between a situation and a new neuron

Prerequisite: the declared pair must differ somewhere measured before anything else

The unit is the declared pair, two situations that share the present input and differ only in context, and the measure is how far apart they are anywhere in the network, relative to their own size. Asking whether the answer already moves refuses precisely the case a structural repair exists for: a distinction that is present in the network and has no path to the declared answer. Two diagnostics are reported and not gated on: the aggregate spread across all situations, which can move while every pair stands still, and the whole-state collision, which is 0.9997 here and gives no support to the idea that the whole state separates by construction.

Same pairs, three gates: 0.0083 measured at the answer (refuses, 1200 ticks), 0.0316 to 0.1228 measured over the state (ninety attempts), and 0.065 to 0.106 on the unit-test substrate.

Demand: indistinguishability is the signal Rule 1

Two situations that the world declares different, share the present input, and produce answers whose absolute cosine exceeds the cap, constitute a capacity signal. The signal is a witness, not a loss: it names the pair that is being confused, and that pair is what the generator wires against.

Cap 0.85. The worm graph delivers 0.9979 to 1.0000 at every scanned point, so the signal fires on everything and therefore names nothing.

Evidence: one collision is noise Rule 2

A single collision can come from a settling failure or a transient. Growth is attempted only after a minimum number of collisions have been observed and a minimum number of ticks have passed, which keeps the structure layer from chasing instantaneous state.

Minimum evidence three collisions, with a tick separation, and a verdict margin of 0.02 on the cosine.

Growth: a low-dimensional generator, not a dense search Rule 3

Each event appends a few neurons. Partners are drawn from the cells that carry the difference between the colliding answers and their one-hop neighbourhood, the excitatory fraction is preserved, the reverse edge is added with a probability so the equilibrium contrast contract survives, and each new neuron projects into the readout with a probability. The generator has thirteen genes, so structural plasticity cannot degrade into implicit weight learning.

Thirteen genes against 3,137 directed synapse classes: the parameter count sits four orders below the graph it can touch.

Verdict: rollback unless the confusion falls Rule 4

After growth, the same collisions are measured again, and again means re-settled: the answers of the stored situations are recomputed on the current graph. A comparison against stored answers cannot see the growth at all, because those answers belong to the graph as it was, and a gate written that way rolls every attempt back by construction. The connectome and the efficacy array are then restored from the snapshot unless the collision falls by the margin, and the attempt is recorded as a failure. A growth that does not pay is a measurement, not a mistake to hide.

Ninety of ninety attempts rolled back across three declared readouts, at about one millisecond of rebuild each, with the receipts in docs/receipts/. The first version of this gate compared stored answers and reported an unchanged collision for every attempt: a gate that cannot see the intervention turns a negative result into an artifact of the gate.

Measurements

Four measurements on the released library, one laptop CPU

Settlement costworm at 302 / 3,137 and sparse graphs to 100,000

1.76 ns per synapse in the sparse regime, and 8.7 ns at 302 neurons where per-sweep overhead dominates, so the control frequency of a settling brain is bounded by one over synapses times nanoseconds times sweeps. A sparse 10,000-neuron graph settles in 6.48 ms and a 100,000-neuron graph in 77.4 ms. The worm graph needs five times as many sweeps as a random graph of the same size and degree at the same tolerance, and that difference is measured in the spectrum rather than assumed: the settling majorant contracts at 0.931 on the worm and 0.618 on the random graph, and a contraction-rate account predicts a sweep ratio of 5.6 against the measured 5.0.

Transport pathchosen by size, not by density

The library picks its transport by a size test, so a 3.4 percent dense graph pays a block array twenty-nine times larger than its edge count. On the worm graph the sparse path is 1.78 times faster end to end, while on a 32 percent dense graph the blocked path is faster. Two defaults matter at scale: the default constructors wire at about 30 percent density independent of size, and a batch size above one builds a temporary array of the full edge count.

Certificatestate-dependent, not worst case

The global contraction bound multiplies the largest absolute row sum by the largest activation slope and gives 88.0 on the worm graph at the library's default efficacy, which certifies no budget at all. The absolute-value majorant of the settling map evaluated with the slopes of the current state has certified contraction 0.529065 there and 0.931159 at the declared row-normalised operating point, with the witness inequality (Kolmogorov, Perron witness, outward-rounded) asserted rather than assumed, and the worst of a twelve-state scanned domain is 0.941. The Gershgorin row criterion exceeds one everywhere, so the certificate is carried by the certified contraction and not by the row sum. The same quantity is the precondition for reading the free and nudged contrast as a gradient, so it is measured at run time rather than assumed.

The regimethe answer has to be able to show an added input

Twenty-four regimes were screened, from row sum 0.5 to 4 and input scale 1 to 10, with and without per-neuron heterogeneity, reporting the answer's mean potential, its mean local slope and its collision. The slope is the quantity that decides whether a repair can be visible at all: a cell below threshold and a cell at the ceiling are equally deaf to a new input, and the band between them is narrow. One hundred and twenty further growth attempts inside the band where the gain is not zero, plus sixty with per-neuron heterogeneity: none kept, the collision unmoved.

The unit testa distinction with no path to the answer

Two input cells are appended to the wiring and driven as the context, reaching nothing. The stored states then differ by 6.5 to 10.6 percent, so the prerequisite passes on its own terms, while the collision at the readout stays at 1.000. The generator may read exactly those cells and project into the answer: sixty attempts over four new-synapse scales, excitatory and inhibitory, all rolled back, the collision unchanged to three decimals. The reason is measured and local, and it was not what we first assumed: screening the regime showed the answer's cells were not at the ceiling but below threshold, where the rectified sigmoid has almost no slope, so a new input is equally invisible. Both failure modes are reported.

The ceilingwhat the most favourable intervention achieves

Twenty direct edges from the two context cells into the ten readout cells, with their weight swept over two orders of magnitude, move the collision by at most 0.0034, five times less than the 0.02 margin a growth must clear, and a hand-built repair neuron that reads the context and writes to the readout does not help at all. With the same budget of thirty attempts, partners drawn from the difference-carrying neighbourhood and partners drawn uniformly produce the same change, +1.0e-4, and growing nothing produces exactly zero. So the negative result belongs to the substrate and its declared readout rather than to the generator, and the generator's targeting is not shown to matter here.

Continuous trackinga negative control result

A composed brain of 140 neurons in a closed loop on a second-order plant reduced mean absolute error by about 13 percent over 6,000 ticks, never beat the frozen baseline on the in-band metric, and collapsed to a single action level 70 to 84 percent of the time in the last quarter. The structural reason is a credit window of twelve steps against a closed-loop time constant of 50 to 100 steps. This is consistent with the library's own control demo, which learns a consequence model and leaves the controller supplied.

Prerequisite scan

Forty-five operating points, nine situations, one direction

Five row sums, a gain on one cell class or none, four gain scales, minus the fifteen cells where nothing is gained, and nine situations: three single senses and six combinations of an odour with a pain level. Spread is the mean over readout cells of the maximum minus the minimum activation across situations. Collision is the largest absolute cosine between two situations that share the present input and differ only in context, which is the pair that growth is supposed to separate.

Four of the forty-five configurations collapse exactly, to a spread of zero on all nine situations, which indicates a fixed point dominated by the recurrent dynamics rather than by the input. Ten declared readouts were scanned at the best point, from the locomotion readout through the sensory, interneuron and motor populations to a random ten-cell set and the whole state of 302 cells: none separates the pairs, the smallest collision over the family is 0.9983, so the failure belongs to the substrate and its operating point rather than to one declaration. The declared-pair motion does vary across the family, from 0.019 for motor cells to 0.123 for a random ten-cell set, and that variation is what decides whether the growth layer has anything to work on. The answer matrix at the best point makes the failure precise: a leading singular value sixty times the second, 99.95 percent of the energy in one direction, a rank-one residual of 2.3 percent, and at most 1.45 degrees between the answers that must be told apart. This is a negative result about the pair of a measured worm wiring and a rate model at a declared operating point, and not a claim about connectome substrates in general. The remedy has three candidates in ascending cost: a different declared readout, a dynamics with heterogeneous cells and per-type parameters, or a gain selected by protocol against held-out physiological facts, which is how the library's own fly guide repairs the same failure at a cosine of 0.98.

Scope

What this work claims, and what it does not

Claimed

  • A growth contract with a prerequisite gate, an evidence gate, and an outcome gate that restores the previous graph when growth does not pay.
  • A low-dimensional structural generator whose partner choice comes from the difference between the colliding answers and whose new neurons must reach the readout.
  • A state-dependent contraction certificate that yields a settling budget on a measured connectome where the global worst-case bound does not apply.
  • A prerequisite measurement, negative, for the pair of the measured 302-neuron wiring and a rate model at forty-five operating points.
  • Settlement, transport, certificate and continuous-control measurements on the released library, each with the probe file that produced it.

Not claimed

  • No growth has been kept. Nothing here shows that structural growth improves anything, only the contract that would measure it and the prerequisite that currently blocks it.
  • No claim that the worm graph is an unsuitable substrate in general. The negative result is specific to the declared readout, the rate model, and the scanned operating points.
  • No scaling law from neuron count to capability. Structure and capability are separate questions.
  • No gradient contract for directed wiring. The library's own documentation states that arbitrary directed imports need not satisfy it, so the growth layer does not assume one.
  • No behaviour, no embodiment and no sensory front end. The senses and the outcome are declared by the world, which the receipt records as supplied.

The network is built on the released Cadence library, whose settling is trained by the contrast of a free and a nudged fixed point, the two-phase form of equilibrium propagation (Scellier and Bengio, 2017) and of contrastive Hebbian learning (Movellan, 1990), with later work on the finite-nudge bias and on Jacobian conditioning. The wiring is the openworm c302 reconstruction of the hermaphrodite. The measuring discipline, protocols written before the run and receipts that separate nominal from executed scale, is adopted from the open fly connectome projects, whose published attempts to drive a body from a measured wiring without a supplied controller are the closest neighbours of this work.
